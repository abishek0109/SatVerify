🏛️ System Architecture & Scientific Rigor

SatVerify enforces the Model Honesty Principle: generic ImageNet neural networks without verified satellite-domain deepfake fine-tuning are never passed off as authentic detectors. The pipeline combines physics-based signal processing, differential compression residuals, and micro-texture entropy into an explainable tri-state decision.

Error Level Analysis (ELA)

Recompresses images at JPEG quality 90 to evaluate residual divergence Δ(x,y) = |I - I_recomp| × 10. Homogeneous sensors degrade uniformly; spliced generative patches leave elevated residual variances.

2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)

Transforms spatial pixels into the frequency domain. Natural terrain follows a 1/fα power roll-off. Generative AI upsamplers leave periodic checkerboard spikes and unnatural high-frequency energy floors.

Local Binary Patterns (LBP)

Extracts 8-neighbor circular micro-texture codes. Measures regional Shannon entropy to catch cloned canopy stamps or smooth synthetic inpainting across forest borders.
